Rolter's identity is one idea carried through everything: a cross-stitch. The
mark is stitched, the ornament is stitched, and the two reds are the ones a
folkloric embroidery actually uses. This page is the contract for anyone
placing that identity somewhere — a README, a conference slide, an integration
listing, a blog post about running Rolter.

Everything here is already in the repository. The mark is `assets/logo.svg`,
the colours are tokens in `ui/src/index.css`, and the type ships from
`node_modules`. Nothing needs downloading, and nothing here resolves to a CDN:
Rolter has to run air-gapped, and so does anything that represents it.

## The mark

<img src="/logo.svg" alt="The Rolter mark" width="96" height="96" noZoom />

The mark is a cross-stitch diamond: **29 rounded squares** on a 7-unit grid
inside a `63 × 63` viewBox, in two reds. The bright red draws the outer
lattice and the single centre stitch; the darker red fills the ring between
them. That split is what makes it read as embroidery rather than as a pixel
grid, so neither red may be dropped or recoloured independently.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **File** | `assets/logo.svg` (also vendored at `user-docs/logo.svg` and `ui/public/logo-mark.svg`) |
| **Geometry** | 29 rects, `6.2 × 6.2` with a `1` corner radius, pitch 7, `viewBox="0 0 63 63"` |
| **Colours** | 17 stitches `#ff4017`, 12 stitches `#b41d21` |
| **Background** | none — the mark is transparent and takes the surface under it |

### Clear space

Keep **one stitch pitch** of empty space on every side — that is `7` units in
the mark's own coordinates, or **⅑ of the rendered width**. At a 63 px mark
that is 7 px; at a 180 px mark it is 20 px. Nothing enters that margin: no
wordmark, no rule, no card edge, no other logo.

The clear space is a minimum, not a target. On a title slide or a social
preview, give it far more.

### Minimum size

**24 px** for the mark on screen, **8 mm** in print. Below that the 0.8-unit
gaps between stitches close up and the diamond fuses into a blob — the one
thing the mark is not allowed to become. Where you need something smaller,
use the favicon (`ui/public/favicon.ico` / `favicon.svg`), which is drawn for
that size, rather than shrinking the mark further.

### Light and dark

`user-docs/docs.json` points `logo.light` and `logo.dark` at the same
`/logo.svg`, and that is deliberate, not an oversight. The mark is
red-on-transparent, and both reds carry their shape on a light and a dark
surface:

| Stitch colour | On `#ffffff` | On `--surface-base` `#111113` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#ff4017` `--red-500` | 3.50:1 | 5.38:1 |
| `#b41d21` `--red-folk` | 6.69:1 | 2.82:1 |

The folk red is the weaker of the two on dark, but it never carries the
silhouette: it only fills stitches held inside the bright-red lattice, so the
mark's outline is a 5.38:1 shape on the dashboard surface. There is therefore
no `logo-dark.svg`, and adding one would fork a file that does not need
forking. If a future mark ever puts `--red-folk` on the outer edge, that
conclusion changes and the files split.

Do not place the mark on a mid-tone: between roughly `#606060` and `#a0a0a0`
both reds fall under 3:1. Put it on a light surface, on the graphite family,
or in its own clear-space panel.

## Colour

Two reds, and they are not interchangeable.

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--red-500` | `#ff4017` | The **accent**. Interactive and attention colour: the active nav marker, a focus accent, the mark's lattice, the one rule on a hero. |
| `--red-folk` | `#b41d21` | The **folkloric** red. Ornament only: the вышивка rule, the mark's inner stitches, a chart series. Never a label, never a link. |

`--red-folk` is `2.82:1` on the dashboard surface, which is under the 4.5:1
floor for text. When brand red has to *be* text, use `--red-folk-text`
(`#ff5a3c`, 6.09:1) — the same family lifted until it reads. The full set,
including `--red-600`, `--red-700`, `--red-tint` and `--red-tint-strong`, is
in `ui/src/index.css`.

Red is a seasoning. On any given surface it should account for a few percent
of the pixels: one accent, one ornament, and graphite for everything else.
A wall of `#ff4017` is off-brand even though every pixel of it is on-palette.

The neutral ground is the zinc ramp — `#111113` (`--surface-base`), `#18181b`
(`--surface-app`), `#1f1f23`, `#27272a` — with `#fafafa` for primary text.
Take these from the tokens; never re-type a hex.

## The вышивка ornament

`.vyshivka-rule` in `ui/src/index.css` is the cross-stitch band: a 10 px
checkerboard of two offset 45° gradients in `--red-folk`. `.vyshivka-dots` is
its quieter dotted sibling, for dividers.

It is a **ceremonial** device. It marks a threshold — a moment where somebody
arrives, is welcomed, or is handed something — and it loses all of that if it
turns into a decorative border.

**Appropriate**

- Ceremonial surfaces: the sign-in card (`ui/src/pages/Login.tsx`) and the
  invitation acceptance card (`ui/src/pages/AcceptInvite.tsx`), where it runs
  full-bleed across the top of the card at full strength.
- Brand surfaces: a title slide, a README header, the social preview image —
  one band at one edge.
- As a whisper elsewhere: `ScreenHeader` runs it at `0.28` opacity, and
  `EmptyState` uses a 64 px stub of it. Both are deliberately faint.

**Not appropriate**

- More than one or two instances on a screen. It is punctuation, not texture.
- Around a data surface: tables, charts, log drawers, forms, dialogs. It
  competes with the thing the operator is reading.
- As a page background, a full-screen tile, or a frame around content.
- Recoloured. It is `--red-folk`, always. A green or blue вышивка is not the
  ornament with a different hue, it is a different ornament.
- Animated, rotated, or scaled off the 10 px tile.

<Note>
  "Вышивка" is Russian for embroidery. The class name is kept in the original
  because the motif is a specific folk tradition rather than a generic
  cross-hatch, and naming it that way is a reminder of where it came from.
</Note>

## Type

**Geist** for everything, **Geist Mono** for anything a machine wrote: model
ids, keys, JSON, logs, CLI output.

Both ship as `@fontsource-variable/geist` and `@fontsource-variable/geist-mono`
and bundle into `ui/dist/assets/*.woff2` at build time. They are never fetched
from Google Fonts or any other font service — that would break the air-gapped
guarantee on the first page load. If you are producing an asset outside this
repository, take the `.woff2` files from `node_modules/@fontsource-variable/`
and embed them.

The stacks are `--font-sans` and `--font-mono`. Fall back to the system UI
sans and to `ui-monospace` only where Geist genuinely cannot be embedded;
never substitute Inter, Roboto or Arial, which flatten the wordmark's
distinctive `t` and `r`.

The wordmark is the word **rolter**, set in Geist SemiBold, lowercase, with
tight tracking (`-0.04em` at display sizes). It is lowercase everywhere,
including at the start of a sentence in UI copy and in the browser tab title.
Prose in this documentation capitalises it as "Rolter" for readability; the
mark and the wordmark do not.

## Social preview

`user-docs/images/og.png` is the 1200 × 630 preview used for the documentation
site and the repository. Its source is `assets/og.svg`, and
`ui/scripts/render-og.ts` rasterises it with the bundled Chromium and the
bundled Geist, so the PNG can be regenerated byte-for-byte from any checkout:

```bash
cd ui && bun scripts/render-og.ts
```

Edit the SVG, re-render, and commit both. The PNG is also vendored at
`ui/public/og.png` so the dashboard's own `og:image` resolves locally instead
of pointing at a hosted URL.

## Don'ts

- Do not recolour the mark, invert it, or render it in a single red.
- Do not add a gradient, a shadow, a glow, a stroke or a bevel to it.
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch or squash it — scale uniformly or not at all.
- Do not put it on a busy photograph, a mid-tone grey, or a red background.
- Do not enclose it in a circle, a rounded square or any other badge; the
  diamond silhouette *is* the containment.
- Do not rebuild it from the stitches into a different arrangement, and do not
  animate the stitches individually.
- Do not set the wordmark in a font other than Geist, and do not capitalise it.
- Do not lock the mark to another logo without the full clear space between
  them, and do not build a combined "Rolter × …" lockup.
- Do not use the mark to imply that Rolter endorses your product. Describing
  an integration ("works with Rolter") is fine; a partner badge is not.
- Do not hot-link any of these assets from a CDN. Copy them into your project.

## Licence

The Rolter source is open, and the mark is not the source. Use it to refer to
Rolter — to link to it, document it, or say your project integrates with it —
following this page. Do not use it as your own product's mark, as part of one,
or in a way that suggests an official relationship. If you are unsure, open an
issue on [the repository](https://github.com/rolter-ai/rolter) and ask.
